Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shopping with us.

Orders & Payment

How do I place an order?
Add the pieces you love to your bag, then tap Send Order on Instagram or Send Order by Email. You can also DM us on Instagram or visit the boutique. We'll reply to confirm your order.
How do I pay?
Once we confirm your order is available, we'll send you the payment details. No payment is taken on this website.
Can I change or cancel my order?
Yes, as long as it hasn't been sent out yet. Message us on Instagram or by email as soon as possible with your order details.
Do the pieces sell out?
Many of our pieces are available in limited quantities. We'll always confirm availability before you pay, and follow us on Instagram to be first to see new drops.

Delivery

Where do you deliver?
We deliver across Lagos, to every state in Nigeria, and internationally on request.
How long does delivery take?
Lagos orders usually arrive in 1–2 business days, and orders to other states in 3–5 business days after payment is confirmed. See Shipping & Returns for details.
How much is delivery?
The delivery fee depends on your location and we confirm it with your order, before you pay.
Can I pick up my order in store?
Yes. Choose store pick-up when you send your order and collect it from our boutique in Opebi, Ikeja during opening hours.

Returns & Exchanges

Can I return or exchange an item?
Unworn items with their tags attached can be exchanged or returned for store credit within 7 days of delivery. Read the full returns policy.
What if my item arrives damaged or wrong?
We're so sorry! Message us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo and we'll put it right as quickly as possible.

Sizing & Products

How do I find my size?
Use our size guide. If you're between sizes or unsure, DM us your measurements and we'll recommend the best fit.
Do the colours look the same in real life?
We do our best to show every piece accurately, but colours can look slightly different on different screens. Ask us for extra photos or a video before ordering.
How should I care for my pieces?
Each product page lists its care instructions under Details & Care. When in doubt, wash cold and dry flat.

Still have a question?

We're happy to help. DM is the fastest way to reach us.
