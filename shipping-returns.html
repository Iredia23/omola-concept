Delivery

Orders are packed within 1 business day of payment being confirmed. Orders confirmed after 2PM, or on a Sunday or public holiday, are packed the next business day.

Delivery times are estimates and may be longer during sales, holidays or bad weather. We'll send you tracking or rider details as soon as your order is on its way.

Returns & Exchanges

We want you to love what you buy. If something isn't right, you can exchange it or return it for store credit within 7 days of delivery, as long as:

  • the item is unworn, unwashed and in its original condition, with all tags attached;
  • it was not bought on final sale; and
  • it is not earrings, underwear or swimwear, which cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.

How to return or exchange

  1. Message us on Instagram or by email with your name, order details and the item you want to return.
  2. We'll confirm your return and tell you where to send it, or you can drop it off at the boutique.
  3. Once we receive and check the item, we'll send your exchange or issue store credit.

Unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong one, the cost of sending a return is paid by the customer.

Damaged or Wrong Items

If your order arrives damaged, faulty or isn't what you ordered, message us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo. We'll arrange a replacement, exchange or refund at no extra cost to you.
