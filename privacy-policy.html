Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Omola Concept ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website or shop with us, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • When you order or contact us: the details you choose to send us by Instagram, email or in store, such as your name, phone number, email address, delivery address and the items you want.
  • Your shopping bag: the items you add to your bag are saved only in your own browser (using "local storage") so they are still there when you come back. They are not sent to us until you choose to send your order.

This website has no user accounts and does not take payments, so we never see or store your card details on it.

How we use your information

  • to confirm, prepare and deliver your order;
  • to reply to your questions and provide customer service;
  • to handle returns and exchanges; and
  • to keep records we are required to keep by law.

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with people who help us run the business, such as delivery riders and couriers, and only as much as they need to do their job.

Third-party services

This website loads fonts, icons, styles, images and maps from other companies (Google Fonts, Google Maps, Font Awesome, Tailwind CSS and Unsplash). When your browser loads them, those companies may receive technical information such as your IP address. Messages you send us on Instagram are also covered by Instagram's own privacy policy.

How long we keep information

We keep order and contact details only as long as we need them for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. You can clear your shopping bag at any time from the bag page or by clearing your browser data.

Contact us

For any privacy question or request, email us at girlthat612@gmail.com or message us on Instagram.
