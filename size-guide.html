Size Guide

Find your perfect fit.

How to Measure

Use a soft tape measure and keep it snug but not tight

1

Bust

Measure around the fullest part of your bust, keeping the tape level under your arms.

2

Waist

Measure around the narrowest part of your waist, usually just above your belly button.

3

Hips

Stand with your feet together and measure around the fullest part of your hips.

4

Feet

Stand on paper, mark your heel and longest toe, and measure the distance between them.
